Bumperbot, chapter 25: ros2_control in Gazebo

Making the robot drivable, one file at a time

Until now the Bumperbot could be seen and simulated, but nothing could turn its wheels. This chapter adds ros2_control. The new code is small. The hard part is that the files point at each other, so this page builds the picture one step at a time, in the order the work is done.

Each step shows one file, explains every line of it, and only then shows how it connects to the files already seen. Every connection can be clicked to see the two lines side by side.
