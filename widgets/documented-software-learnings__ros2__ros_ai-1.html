The idea: subtract the first turtle’s coordinates from the second turtle’s coordinates.

Tx = x2 - x1
Ty = y2 - y1
T(1 to 2) = (x2 - x1, y2 - y1)

Example: Turtle 1 = (5.54, 5.54) and Turtle 2 = (1.00, 4.00). Press “Load the example” above to see it.

Tx = 1.00 - 5.54 = -4.54
Ty = 4.00 - 5.54 = -1.54

A negative Tx means Turtle 2 is to the left of Turtle 1. A negative Ty means it is below Turtle 1. The exact values depend on where the turtles are.

Important distinction: this is the coordinate difference in the Turtlesim world frame. We are not rotating the vector into Turtle 1’s local frame, so the turtles’ headings play no part here.
