Raman Research and Innovation: Company Website and Admin Panel

A modern website for an Industry 4.0 and 5.0 technology company, with a password-free Google-login admin panel to edit products, portfolio, team, partners and SEO and to read enquiries, all on Firebase's free plan.

Introduction

Developed the website for Raman Research and Innovation Pvt. Ltd. as a prototype. The current version serves as a template, with an admin panel planned to let the owner update website content, manage every section, and view contact enquiries. The entire system runs on Firebase's free tier.

Visit the live website

What it had to do

  • Look credible: a firm that sells engineering skill needs a professional site that explains its domains and shows real work, not a "coming soon" page or a generic template.
  • Be run by the company itself: products, case studies, team members and partner logos are edited by the owner, without paying a developer for every change and without code.
  • Cost nothing to run, and be secure: it stays inside Firebase's free plan with no card on file, with real security rules, strict security headers and admin access locked to the owner's Google account.

The products, case studies and team shown on the site are still sample content, until the company adds its own through the admin panel.

  • Live site: raman-research.web.app

How it works

  • Static pages, live content: every page ships with built-in content, so it is never blank. Live content from Firestore is then laid on top, but only for collections that actually have content.
  • Contact form: each enquiry is written to the enquiries collection; if Firebase is not set up, the form opens an email instead.
  • Admin panel: signs the owner in with Google, checks the email against the allowlist, then edits the collections and the SEO settings directly.
  • Security rules: anyone can read the content, only the admin can change it, and anyone may send an enquiry that only the admin can read.
  • Hosting: clean URLs and seven security headers on every response.

Public features

Home

The hero shows "Your Idea. Our Technology." over a live network of nodes drawn on a canvas: the nodes drift, link to their neighbours and react to the cursor. Below it come the company introduction with four stat tiles and a grid of six domain cards.

Further down: four "Why choose us" cards, a scrolling ribbon of technologies (ESP32, STM32, Raspberry Pi, LoRaWAN, MQTT, TensorFlow Lite and more) that pauses on hover, and a scrolling strip of partner logos. The partner strip comes from the database, turns each logo from grey to colour on hover, and hides itself when there are no partners. Testimonial cards and a "Have an idea? Let's build it together." banner close the page.

About, domains and services

  • About: the company overview, Vision and Mission cards, and a team grid filled from the database.
  • Domains: a featured Industry 4.0 and 5.0 block and six detailed domain cards.
  • Services: five core services (idea validation, product design, prototype development, IoT and automation solutions, technical consulting), a four-step process (Discover, Design, Develop, Deliver) and six frequently asked questions that open and close.

Products and portfolio

Both pages are filled from the database. Each product card has a photo, a status badge (Deployed, Field-Tested, Pilot or Prototype), domain tags and spec chips. Each portfolio case study is a numbered row with its sector, type, a photo and a short story: challenge, what was built, outcome. A filter bar (IoT, Automation, Agritech, Robotics and AI, Smart Gadgets, Product Development) shows and hides case studies instantly.

Contact, 404 and search engines

The contact form asks for name, organisation (optional), email, phone, the idea's title and a short description, then shows a success message. Unknown addresses get an on-brand 404 page, "This page went off the grid". Every page also sets its own title, description, social preview tags and structured data, and the site has a sitemap and robots file.

On a phone

Every page folds into one column, and the navigation becomes a menu button. Elements fade in as they scroll into view, unless the visitor has asked for reduced motion.

Admin panel

The admin lives at its own unlisted address, is kept out of search engines and has one role: admin. Sign-in is Google only; an email that is not on the allowlist is signed straight back out, and the database rules enforce the same allowlist on the server.

  • Products, portfolio, team and partners: add, edit, delete and reorder each item, with all its fields (for a product: title, status, description, tags, spec chips and photo).
  • SEO settings: business name, description, keywords, contact details, address, social profile links (which also switch on the footer icons), search engine verification codes, and a title and description for each of the seven pages.
  • Enquiries inbox: the newest enquiries first, each marked unread or handled, with Reply by email, Mark handled and Delete.
  • Photo upload with crop and zoom: every photo field opens a crop window (drag to move, a slider to zoom, a round frame for team photos). The picture is compressed in the browser and saved. Logos skip the crop and keep their transparent background.
  • Two modes: "Live, Firestore" for the real site, and a local file mode that edits a draft and exports it as a file, with a one-time import that copies the built-in content into Firestore.

Problems solved

Photo uploads without paid storage

Firebase Storage now needs the paid plan. Instead of asking the owner for a card, the admin got a hand-written cropper: the browser crops, zooms and compresses each picture and stores it right inside the database document. The result is a real "upload, crop, save" experience on the free plan, with a separate "fit" path for logos that keeps their transparency.

Google sign-in would let in the whole world

With Google sign-in, anyone with a Gmail account can sign in. An email allowlist closes this in two places: the admin page, and more importantly the database rules, which also insist on Google as the sign-in method so nobody can pose as the owner another way. Live tests confirmed that visitors can neither read enquiries nor change content.

A strict security policy blocked inline scripts

The Content Security Policy forbids inline JavaScript to protect against script injection. The admin's script moved into its own file and the structured data is added as data blocks the policy allows. The site now has no inline scripts and seven security headers.

Clean, professional addresses

Links like /about.html became /about: clean URLs in hosting, every internal link rewritten, canonical and social preview addresses added, and old .html addresses redirected to the clean ones.

A logo strip without gaps

With only a few partners, the scrolling strip showed an empty gap. The strip now repeats the logos until they fill the screen, then doubles that block, so the loop never breaks whether there are 3 partners or 30.

A contact form that cannot be abused

Anyone can send the form, so the rule for enquiries allows only new entries, with a fixed set of fields and a size limit on each. The collection cannot be misused as free storage, and the enquiries stay private to the admin.

Numbers

  • 9 pages: home, about, domains, products, portfolio, services, contact, 404 and the admin.
  • 6 Firestore collections and 6 JavaScript files.
  • 7 technology domains and 5 core services presented.
  • 7 security headers on every response.
