Problem Statement (Given by Teknofest juries)

The turbulence of the fuels in the fuel tanks of the liquid propellant rocket engines throughout the flight puts chaotic loads on both the fuel tanks and other structural elements, in addition to causing instability in rocket engine performance. Teams may opt to design an experimental setup for the observation and testing this phenomenon. The teams can prove mission success by transmitting the data they obtain from the turbulence test setup (based on measurements made throughout the flight) to the team’s ground station.

What We Built (Our Payload Concept)

We designed a 4-kg scientific payload that simulates a miniature fuel tank and measures:

ParameterMeasured using
Fuel turbulence / sloshing force3× Load cells (HX711)
Slosh movement inside the tankIMU inside a floating ball (MPU6050)
Rocket motion orientationBNO085 IMU
Altitude vs slosh turbulenceBMP390 Barometric Sensor
GPS coordinates during flightNeo-6M GPS
