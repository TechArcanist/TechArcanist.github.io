From identifying problems to building prototypes

Yenepoya Institute of Technology (YIT) · 2025-26
Role: Program Mentor

Overview

I worked as an Program Mentor for the Innovation & Design Thinking Program, supporting students as they moved from identifying problems to designing, building, and presenting their own solutions.

The program combined design thinking with hands-on technical development, covering areas such as:

  • Design Thinking & Problem Analysis
  • Arduino, Electronics & Sensors
  • CAD & 3D Printing
  • PCB Designing
  • Prototyping & Iteration
  • Project Mentoring & Troubleshooting

My role extended beyond individual sessions. I worked with student teams during project development and prototyping, helping them overcome technical challenges, improve their solutions, and prepare for their final demonstrations.

From Idea → Prototype → Demonstration

The experience gave me an opportunity to not only teach technical concepts, but also guide students through the process of turning an idea into something they could actually build.

The complete details of the program, activities, sessions, mentoring, and student projects are documented in the reports below. These documents provide a deeper look into the work carried out throughout the program and the projects developed by the students.
