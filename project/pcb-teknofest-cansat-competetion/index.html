My Contribution (volunteer role)

I joined only as a technical helper for PCB designing.

Responsibility AreaMy Contribution
Schematic ReviewReviewed connections, power domains, and pin mapping; checked that the design followed proper electrical rules.
PCB Routing & LayoutHelped with routing traces and placement of key components to ensure a compact and manufacturable layout.
DRC & Gerber PreparationPerformed final design rule check and generated Gerber files for PCB fabrication.
External sensor expansion (team decision)BNO085, BMP390, and UV (LTR390) sensors were added later by the team.

My Learnings

  • PCB design is not component placement, it’s communication planning. Making sure every module communicates reliably (correct power domain, routing discipline, noise avoidance) matters more than adding features.
  • Efficient placement is a skill, not luck. Learned how to arrange many sensors and connectors in limited PCB space while still keeping routing clean and noise-free.
